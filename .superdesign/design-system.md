# Orderia Business Order — Design System

## Brand
- **Product:** Orderia Business — B2B foodservice wholesale ordering
- **Logo:** `public/icon.png` (orange mark) + wordmark “Orderia Business” in navy
- **Colors:** Navy `#002d62` (ink), Orange `#f58220` (primary / CTA), white paper, cool muted greys
- **Tone:** Utilitarian B2B marketplace — dense, operational, not consumer e‑commerce fluff
- **Anti-slop:** Avoid purple gradients, oversized rounded-2xl card stacks, pill-everything chrome, floating badge stickers on media

## Typography
- Sans: Inter / Geist
- Headings: navy, semibold, tracking-tight
- Captions: 11px uppercase tracking-wider for field legends (storefront panels)

## Header (current)
- Sticky white/95 blur bar, h-16, max-width 1440
- Left: hamburger (mobile) · logo 36px + wordmark
- Center: delivery address picker · pill search
- Right: Sign in / Sign up (or user menu) · locale · favourites · cart badge
- Mobile: second row with delivery + search

## Interaction
- Primary buttons: orange fill, white text
- Ghost/nav icons: navy on muted hover
- Focus: orange ring
